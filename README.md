One picture summing up the world's news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026**:
33 stories from 7 regions and 14 fields — a Russian drone crossing into
Poland overnight, Asia's biggest stock rally in months, and the youngest
world chess final ever starting in Geneva — plus a UTC timeline, six big
numbers, a world map with numbered pins, a numbered index, and a DATE CHECK
box naming the stories that belong to other days.

`render.py` draws the picture with Pillow (no network, no external files,
deterministic). `main.ts` copies the rendered `entry.png` to the output
directory and verifies it is a 1024×1024 PNG under 1 MiB.

Run: `python3 render.py` — writes `entry.png` next to this file.
